## Non-goals — considered and ruled out

Recorded so future sessions stop re-proposing them.

- ~~**Full generous-budget union-across-runs across all 11 fileable dicts** — cut to three dicts (D7).
  Tier-A precision is near-zero on mature dictionaries; recall gain must be *measured* before any
  scale-up is reconsidered.~~
  **No longer a non-goal — FUNDED 26-07-2026 (MG ruling D9).** The measurement D7 demanded exists
  (H1471: **+81%** recall, **35%** single-run agreement), and the decisive argument turned out not to
  be recall at all:

  > *"Otherwise the bad headwords will spoil the general headword list."* — MG, 26-07-2026

  An uncorrected typo headword does not stay contained in its own dictionary's FILE-FIRST queue. It
  propagates into the **cross-dict union headword list**
  ([union_attestation.py](detectors/union_attestation.py), `HeadwordLists/union/union_headwords.tsv`),
  which the ranker reads to demote suspects "attested across many independent dictionaries". So a typo
  left unfixed **inflates its own attestation count and helps suppress its own detection** — a
  feedback loop that gets worse the longer it runs, and that silently degrades every downstream
  consumer of the union list, not just this repo's queues. That reframes the cost-benefit: the
  near-zero tier-A precision on mature dicts (H2) makes a blanket sweep look like poor value *if the
  only payoff is the handful of corrections*, but the real payoff is keeping contamination out of a
  shared asset. **Scope parameter still open** — the 11 fileable dicts (the original non-goal's scope)
  vs all 33; see the scale-up handoff.

- **Holding the GRETIL typo list back for publication priority** — rejected (D8).
- **Waiting on CORRECTIONS#447 replies as the delivery mechanism** — superseded by batched PRs (D3).
- **Recruiting a human second annotator for A44 during 2026** — stays deferred; the LLM agreement
  study replaces it (D2).
- **Refuted paths, do not re-attempt:** corpus-based tier-C promotion (R-tier ceiling), the vidyut

## Standing constraints (carry into every session)

- **`csl-orig` is read-only to agents** — corrections applied only with explicit M.G. authorization,
  via the canonical workflow (XML validation before commit; no BOMs).
- **Maintainer noise sensitivity** — one consolidated PR per month, batched follow-ups, edit-in-place;
  never per-word issues, never bot-applied edits.
- **Cologne `servepdf` sustained-429s from this IP** — scan fetches from a different network or
  server-side only; polite rates always.
- **The LLM TYPO pass is stochastic** — never blindly re-run or overwrite a committed triage package;
  recall grows by union-across-runs only.
- **Model attribution** — report tier AND exact version (e.g. Opus 4.8 `claude-opus-4-8`) for every
  step of every run, per-phase in multi-agent workflows.
